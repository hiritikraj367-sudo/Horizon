Privacy Policy

This page explains how Home Vexa uses information provided through this storefront.

Information you provide

Checkout collects your name, mobile number, delivery address, city and PIN code. Email is optional and is used to send your order confirmation. Order records include the items ordered, amount, order reference and status.

How we use information

We use order information to process purchases, arrange delivery and respond to customer requests. Delivery providers receive the details needed to deliver your order. Confirmation emails are processed by our hosting provider’s mail service.

Cookies and saved preferences

The website uses session cookies to support checkout and administrator sign-in. Your browser may also store your saved-product preference locally.

Your requests

To ask about your information, correct delivery details or request deletion, contact orders@homevexa.online. Include your order reference so we can locate your record and verify the request. Some records may need to be retained to fulfil orders or applicable obligations.
