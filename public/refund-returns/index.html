Refunds & Returns

If there is a problem with your delivery, contact orders@homevexa.online with your order reference and a description of the issue.

Damaged, incorrect or missing items

Please contact us promptly after delivery. Include photos of the item and packaging where relevant, and keep the packaging while we review your request.

Return requests

Contact us before sending an item back. We will review the issue and confirm the return instructions, any applicable collection arrangements and available resolution. Do not send items to an unconfirmed address.

Refunds and replacements

When a refund or replacement is approved, we will explain the next steps and expected processing time by email. For cash on delivery orders, we will arrange an appropriate refund method directly with you.

Cancellation

For cancellation requests, email your order reference as soon as possible. Cancellation depends on whether the order has been dispatched. This process does not limit applicable consumer rights.
